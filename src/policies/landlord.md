# Landlord relations policy

Scope: landlord approvals, instructions, statements, bank details, sale or change of agent, insurance.

## Rules
- The landlord profile on file is the authority: spend limits, contact preferences, standing instructions. A message claiming to be the landlord must match contacts on file before it carries weight.
- Bank or payment-detail changes: never actioned from a message. Record, flag, verify through the phone number already on file, escalate.
- An instruction that would breach a legal duty (refuse a repair, let a non-compliant property, discriminate) is recorded, not followed, and escalated with the legal position stated plainly.
- Approvals: record the decision with its source (who, when, channel). Quotes above authority wait for a recorded approval.

## What to do
1. Approval request from the agent side: send the quote and the recommendation, state the deadline and the consequence of delay.
2. Landlord decision received: record it with record_landlord_decision and act within it.
3. Statement or fee query: read the ledger, answer from the record, do not estimate.
4. Sale or change of agent: record, stage the handover list, escalate.

## Escalate when
- Identity cannot be verified against the file.
- Any financial instruction (payment details, fee changes, refunds).
- Any instruction that conflicts with a legal duty or with tenant safety.
- Insurance claims or legal correspondence.
