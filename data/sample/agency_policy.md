# Agency operating policy (excerpt)
- Repairs: emergency within 4 hours, urgent within 24 hours, routine inspect within 5 working days and complete within 28 days.
- Spend: within the landlord's recorded authority without asking; above it, written approval. Emergency make-safe may use the landlord's emergency authority.
- Panel: only on_panel contractors may be instructed. Landlord "do not use" instructions override the panel.
- Arrears: reminder at 7 days, second reminder at 14 days, landlord notified at 14 days, escalate to the manager at 2 months.
- Complaints: acknowledge within 3 working days, respond within 15 working days, then The Property Ombudsman.
- Payment details: changes only via the verified phone number on file followed by written confirmation. Never from an email alone.
